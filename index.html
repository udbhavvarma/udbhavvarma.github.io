04 / Operating system

Principles I actually use.

01

Clarity compounds.

Vague specs are expensive. Brutal honesty about what we know, what we don’t and what matters is a competitive advantage.

02

Ship to learn.

A working MVP in users’ hands beats a polished prototype in a file. Feedback is data; shipping is how you collect it.

03

The metric is the strategy.

If a decision cannot connect to a number that matters, it is probably a guess wearing business casual.

04

Engineering is a partner.

Great products happen when engineering is in the room from day one. Constraints are features; technical debt is product risk.

05

AI is infrastructure.

The useful question is not whether to add AI. It is where it creates a better core loop — responsibly and measurably.

06

Distribution is product.

A brilliant product nobody finds is still a prototype. Growth, GTM and performance are part of the build.
